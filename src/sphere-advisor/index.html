Mudae Sphere Advisor

Fill in the commands and get the upgrades based on the community sphere upgrade path

Choose your path

Pick the hardest path you meet the requirements for. You follow its column of the upgrade chart and every column to its left.

1 Send $mmsz=z!

Mudae DMs you every character with spheres in it. Paste the whole DM (all of its messages if it's split).

2 Send $shop

Paste the reply, or type the 10 shop perk levels yourself.

3 Send $wls

4 Enter values

Upgrade plan

Go in order from step 1. Each step shows the spheres a day it adds, from the upgrade chart. Steps marked "waits" need more characters first, so skip them for now.
