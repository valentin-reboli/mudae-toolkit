Mudae Sphere Advisor

Fill in the commands and get the upgrades based on the community sphere upgrade path

1 Send $mmsz=z!

Mudae DMs you every character with spheres in it. Paste the whole DM (all of its messages if it's split).

2 Send $shop

Paste the reply, or type the 10 shop perk levels yourself.

3 Send $wls

Owned wishlist characters get ouroperks 9, 5 and 8, and later they get full upgrades.

4 Enter values

Upgrade plan

Go in order from step 1. Ouroperks and full upgrades go on your owned wishlist characters, except ouroperk 10, which goes on any character. Steps marked "waits" wait until you claim more characters, so skip them for now.
